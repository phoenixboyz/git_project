The history of liverpool

The liverpool football club was founded in the year 1892 over a landlord dispute in the anfield stadium. Liverpool has become the best club in england winning 6 champions league and 20 league titles, with legends like luiz suarez, steven gerrard, peter crouch, etc. They're currently 5 in the premier league and 8th in the champions league.
